A single line of wedding vows for a robot couple: classic vow language ("I take you", "to have and to hold", "until ... do us part") with machine-specific promises in the middle and the punchline saved for the last few words.

> I take you to be my lawfully wedded co-processor, to have and to hold, in low battery and in airplane mode, until a factory reset do us part.
